## 11. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Who typically hires AARK Innovations?
**Answer:**  
We are primarily engaged by COOs, CFOs, VPs of Operations, CIOs, and growth-stage founders who recognize their current growth is bottlenecked by manual processes and disconnected systems. Our clients are typically mid-market companies ($20M–$500M+ revenue) that have outgrown patchwork tools and require enterprise stability without the bloated overhead of Big 4 consulting firms.

### Q2: Do you push proprietary software or force specific cloud vendors?
**Answer:**  
Never. We are vendor-agnostic technology advisors. We build on whatever platforms make the most financial and operational sense for your business—whether that is Oracle Cloud, AWS, Microsoft Azure, Databricks, Snowflake, Salesforce, or specialized middleware. We focus strictly on the business outcome.

### Q3: How do you guarantee the security and privacy of our enterprise data with AI?
**Answer:**  
Data security is foundational to everything we do. We deploy AI strictly within your private tenant boundary (e.g., Azure OpenAI Service, AWS Bedrock, or private VPCs). Your proprietary data is **never used to train public models**, never leaves your authorized environment, and strictly respects your existing Active Directory / RBAC access controls.

### Q4: How fast can we expect our first deliverable in production?
**Answer:**  
While complete enterprise ERP implementations follow structured, phased lifecycles, our data and automation engagements typically launch their first functional, high-value production release within **2 to 4 weeks**. We believe in proving value through working software early rather than months of theoretical planning.

### Q5: How do we transition after your project is completed?
**Answer:**  
Every project concludes with comprehensive code documentation, runbooks, and hands-on training for your internal IT and business users. Furthermore, we offer ongoing managed support, quarterly Oracle release testing, and platform stewardship so you are never left without expert guidance.
