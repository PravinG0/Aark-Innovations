1. Introduction & Scope

AARK Innovations respects the privacy of our clients, prospective clients, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we may collect when you visit https://aarkinn.com (the "Site"), communicate with our team, or evaluate our consulting practices, and how that information is gathered, safeguarded, and utilized.

This policy applies solely to information collected through our public website and introductory correspondence. Client consulting engagements are governed by separate Master Services Agreements (MSAs), Statements of Work (SOWs), and Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).

2. Information We Collect

We collect information only to the extent necessary to conduct legitimate business discussions and deliver professional services:

  1. Voluntarily Provided Inquiry Data: When you submit our contact form, book a diagnostic working session, or email us directly at hello@aarkinn.com, you may provide:
    • Full Name
    • Corporate Work Email Address
    • Company Name
    • Job Title / Role
    • Current System Architecture (e.g., Oracle, Snowflake, AWS)
    • Operational Problem Description & Project Timeline
  2. Technical & Usage Telemetry: When navigating the Site, standard, non-personally identifiable telemetry may be logged by our hosting infrastructure, including IP address, browser type, referring URL, pages viewed, and device parameters. We do not engage in cross-site tracking or third-party behavioral profiling.

3. Purpose & Legal Basis of Processing

We utilize collected information strictly for legitimate commercial purposes:

  • To evaluate technical feasibility and respond directly to your project inquiry.
  • To schedule and conduct introductory architectural working sessions.
  • To provide customized proposals, Statements of Work (SOWs), and cost estimates.
  • To monitor, secure, and optimize the performance and stability of our website.

4. Zero Data Selling & Limited Third-Party Sharing

AARK Innovations does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or business data under any circumstances.

We share data only with trusted infrastructure providers required to operate our digital communications (e.g., enterprise cloud email servers, calendar scheduling systems, and secure web hosting), all of which are bound by strict enterprise confidentiality obligations and industry-standard security protocols.

5. Client Data Security in AI & Consulting Deployments

For prospective clients evaluating our Applied AI, Lakehouse, or Oracle ERP consulting practices:

  • Zero Public Training: We never train public machine learning models on client data or confidential project inquiries.
  • Tenant Isolation: All production AI workflows, RAG architectures, and database integrations are deployed strictly within the client’s designated private cloud tenant boundary (e.g., Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, or private VPCs).
  • Access Controls: Client systems are accessed only under strict Least-Privilege Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC) governed by explicit client authorization.

We retain inquiry details only as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill legitimate commercial evaluation or ongoing business relationships.

  • Request confirmation of what personal information we hold regarding you.
  • Request the prompt correction, updating, or permanent deletion of your inquiry records.
  • Opt out of any further direct correspondence from our team.
